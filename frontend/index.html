The Story

A local eatery on the shore of Baptiste Lake

Baptiste Lake Burger Bar is a casual, cozy roadside spot in South Baptiste, Alberta — the kind of place you bike up to after a morning on the water. The kitchen turns out classic Canadian and North American plates: smash burgers, hand-cut poutine with real cheese curds, and weekend breakfast, all in generous portions.

Indoor booths and a covered outdoor porch make it easy for families and groups to grab a table, and the whole space is wheelchair accessible. Can't stay? Takeout and delivery are always on the table.
